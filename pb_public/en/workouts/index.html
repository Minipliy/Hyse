Experience training together

Workout planner

From your first set to your next record.

Plan routines, log sets on iPhone and Apple Watch, analyse progress, import Hevy data and connect with your gym community.

Features at a glance

Active workout

Plan routines or start empty

Start an empty workout or a saved plan, carry over previous values and adjust exercise order as you train.

Exercise library

Find and understand exercises

Search by muscle group or equipment, use favourites, local exercise images and execution guidance.

Custom exercises

Your own training catalogue

Create private exercises with names, muscles, equipment and an optional photo or execution guide.

Sets

Weight, reps and RPE

Log every set with weight, repetitions and optional RPE. Warm-up, drop-set and failure sets and supersets stay distinguishable.

Rest timer

Timer, sounds and haptics

Adjust rest periods, use the rest timer with selectable Hyse sounds and get a notification when a rest period ends.

Progress

Volume, time and records

Review workout duration, volume, repetitions, personal records, weekly streaks and trained muscle groups in clear statistics.

Muscle analysis

Body views and charts

Analyse sets by muscle, radar and body views, plus top and record exercises across several time ranges.

Monthly reports

A monthly look back

Monthly reports summarise workouts, duration, volume, sets, records, muscle distribution and weekly streaks, with a share card.

Share training

Make progress visible

Share completed workouts and routines as a card or text and choose which sessions are public on your profile.

Hevy import

Bring your history and routines

Import compatible Hevy CSV files for workouts, exercises and body measurements. Hyse creates editable routines from workouts in the past month.

Nearby gyms

Find gyms with Apple Maps

After training, search real nearby gyms sorted by distance when you grant location access.

Gym Communities

Friends, feed and news from your gym.

Search by gym, community or city. Create a community with an image and description, manage member roles, and publish official news or chat in the member channel. Reactions, replies, editing, deletion and reporting keep conversations clear.
