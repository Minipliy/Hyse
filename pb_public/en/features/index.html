Nutrition & wellbeing

Understand what shapes your day.

Calories, macros, barcodes, recipes, local text logging, dynamic targets, water, fasting, streaks and Apple Health in Hyse.

Features at a glance

Daily overview

Calories and nutrients in context

Assign entries to meals, move between dates, and follow calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fibre, sugar, salt and more.

Foods

Search, scan, create your own

Combine local foods with favourites, recent entries, barcodes and the shared catalogue. Add names, brands, serving details and nutrients yourself.

Text logging

Text as a suggestion

Log foods from a description directly on your device. Review detected foods and amounts before adding them to your day.

Recipes

Recalculate any amount

Combine ingredients, maintain portions and preparation steps, and recalculate nutrition for the amount you want.

Quick logging

Quick Entry and batch logging

Save simple entries directly, collect several foods in one list, and edit amounts, units and meals before saving.

Targets

Fixed or dynamic calorie goals

Use a fixed daily target or let Hyse calculate the budget dynamically from your settings and optional activity energy from Apple Health.

Water & fasting

Hydration and fasting in your rhythm

Track water against a personal goal and start common or custom fasting intervals with a saved history.

Body & Health

Weight, steps and energy

Keep a weight history and optionally connect weight, steps, resting energy, active energy and water with Apple Health.

Reminders

Choose helpful nudges

Enable motivation, food, water, planned-training or missing-daily-data reminders and control frequency and language.

Streak rewards

Make consistency visible

Earn ice crystals for your first food entry and workout each day and protect streaks with nutrition or training freezes.

Note: Hyse is a personal tracking tool and does not replace medical advice.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I use Hyse without an account?

Yes. Core features and guest data work locally on your device. An account is optional and syncs selected data.

How does text logging work?

Hyse processes your description on the device. Review and edit the suggestions before saving.

Do I need Apple Health?

No. Apple Health is optional. Hyse works without Health permissions and only uses categories you grant.
