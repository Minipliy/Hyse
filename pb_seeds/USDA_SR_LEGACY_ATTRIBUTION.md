# USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy catalogue

The normalized seed `usda_sr_legacy_foods.json.gz` is derived from the USDA
FoodData Central Standard Reference Legacy April 2018 JSON release.

Source: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/

USDA FoodData Central data are public domain and published under CC0 1.0.
Nutrients and original English descriptions are retained unchanged. German
search/display names were translated locally with the open-source Argos
Translate English–German model; common dish aliases were manually normalized.
